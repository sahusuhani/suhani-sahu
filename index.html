A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I NOTICE THINGS.

probably more than I need to.

I'm a Digital Business and Entrepreneurship student at IIM Bangalore, with a commerce background and a slightly excessive curiosity about how things work.

I'm drawn to marketing, brand identity, consumer behaviour and strategic communication — especially when there is something human hiding underneath all the business language.

I like understanding why people choose what they choose, why some brands stay with us, and why an ordinary idea can become interesting when looked at from a different angle.

I'm still discovering exactly where all of this takes me. For now, I'm happy asking questions, trying things and seeing what sticks.
